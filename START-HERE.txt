SHOP ADS ANALYSER  -  START HERE
====================================================

WHAT THIS IS
    Analyses your Shopee Shop+ Ads keyword exports on this computer.
    Nothing is uploaded anywhere. It works without internet after setup.

WHAT TO DO  (about 15 minutes, once)

    1. Put this ShopAds folder at C:\ShopAds
       Do NOT leave it in Downloads, OneDrive, Desktop or Program Files.
       Windows blocks programs from writing files in those places.

    2. Double-click            Setup.bat
       Wait for it to say "Setup complete."
       If Windows shows a blue warning box, click
       "More info" then "Run anyway".

    3. Double-click            Start-Watcher.bat
       Leave that black window open. It is waiting for you.

    4. Drag your Shopee export into the  drop  folder.

    5. Open the newest file in the  reports  folder.
       It opens in your web browser.

       Every number has a small (i) beside it. Hover it and it
       explains, in plain English, what the number is, how it was
       worked out, why it matters, and what a good value looks
       like. On a phone, tap it. Press Escape to close.
       You do not need to know anything about advertising.

EVERY MONTH AFTER THAT
    Just repeat steps 3, 4 and 5. The tool remembers every export,
    so from your second one onward each report shows what changed.

TO SEE PROFIT, NOT JUST REVENUE
    Open  shopads.config.json  in Notepad. Find "business".
    Fill in your contribution margin (what is left of a sale after
    product cost, shipping, payment fees and returns - 0.32 means
    32%) and your total shop revenue for the period.
    The next report then shows profit on ad spend, your break-even
    ROAS, and TACOS. Keep the punctuation exactly as it is.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
    Double-click            Check-Setup.bat
    It checks everything and tells you what to fix, in plain English.

THE BUTTONS
    Setup.bat           Run once, the first time only.
    Start-Watcher.bat   Your everyday button. Leave it open.
    Analyse-Now.bat     Analyse what is waiting, then close.
                        You can also drag a file straight onto it.
    History.bat         List every export analysed so far.
    Check-Setup.bat     Diagnose problems.

NEEDS
    Python 3.10 or newer, from https://www.python.org/downloads/
    When installing Python, TICK THE BOX that says
    "Add python.exe to PATH" on the very first screen.

More detail is in README.md, which opens in Notepad.
