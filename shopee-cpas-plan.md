# Shopee CPAS Plan: Commercial Ovens and Deep Fryers

Living document. Ask Claude to "update the plan with new results" and paste your numbers into the Results Log. Visual playbook: `shopee-cpas-playbook.html`.

## Context
- Products: commercial ovens, commercial deep fryers (bundles planned)
- Channel: Meta ads via Shopee CPAS, sales happen on Shopee
- Market: assumed Shopee Malaysia, budgets in RM (confirm)
- Last updated: 2026-11-17

## Steps

### Phase 0: Eligibility and access
1. **Ask your Shopee contact: CPAS eligibility, minimum ad spend, reporting access.**
   Why: the whole plan depends on it. If you are not eligible, use link ads (Step 15).
2. **Get Business Manager, Facebook Page, ad account; share partner access with Shopee.**
   Why: CPAS only works when the ad account is linked to Shopee's catalog.
3. **Ask what conversion data Shopee sends back and how often.**
   Why: Meta can only optimise on what Shopee reports, and delays hurt learning.

### Phase 1: Fix the listing (this is your creative)
4. **Main image 1080x1080, clean background, machine fills ~80%, no text.**
   Why: CPAS builds the ad from this image; it is the biggest creative lever.
5. **Add images: food in use, size reference, spec image (litres, kW, voltage).**
   Why: buyers of equipment compare specs and want proof of output.
6. **Titles: key words first, about 35 characters visible. Keep prices and sale prices accurate.**
   Why: titles truncate on cards, and price mismatches get ads rejected.
7. **Create 3 product sets: Ovens, Deep Fryers, Bundles.**
   Why: lets you see which product earns its spend, and match copy to product.

### Phase 2: Build campaigns
8. **C1 Prospecting: Sales objective, catalog, 3 ad sets (one per product set), same audience.**
   Audience: Malaysia, 25 to 55, Advantage+ audience, F&B owner interests as hints only.
   Why: same audience means differences come from the product, not the people. Broad lets Meta find buyers.
9. **3 ads per ad set: catalog carousel, single product with frame, collection with video cover.**
   Why: Meta rewards different creative types; each reaches a different kind of viewer.
10. **Budget example RM30/day per ad set. Under ~RM100/day total, start with 2 ad sets (Ovens, Fryers).**
    Why: high-priced items give few purchases, and Meta needs ~50 purchases/week to exit learning.
11. **Optimise for Purchases, lowest cost. Placements: Advantage+.**
    Why: lowest cost gives the learning phase the most room. Check Reels/Stories accept your format.
12. **Do not edit anything for the first 5 to 7 days.**
    Why: edits reset learning and waste budget.

### Phase 3: Retargeting and events
13. **Week 3: launch C2 Retargeting (added to cart 7d, viewed 14d, exclude buyers), RM45/day.**
    Why: needs enough viewers to exist; warm people convert cheaper. Cap frequency awareness at 4 per 7 days.
14. **12.12 / year-end / pre-CNY: switch on C3 with sale frames, ~3x budget for 3 to 5 days.**
    Why: Shopee traffic and conversion peak on these days; ads ride that demand.

### Phase 4: Parallel test
15. **Run one small link or WhatsApp campaign (10 to 20% of budget) with your own videos.**
    Why: tests hooks freely, catches buyers who want a quote, and is the fallback if CPAS is delayed. Meta cannot see Shopee sales from these, so track in Seller Centre.

### Phase 5: Review loop
16. **Weekly: pause the weakest ad per ad set by cost per purchase; add a new hook.**
    Why: concentrates budget on winners while keeping creative fresh.
17. **Refresh frames and video every 2 to 3 weeks.**
    Why: creative fatigue raises cost per purchase.
18. **Reconcile Meta results against Shopee Seller Centre (orders, GMV, margin after fees and ad spend).**
    Why: Meta attributes only sales tied to ads; Shopee shows real business profit.

## Decision rules
| Metric | Act when | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Cost per purchase | Above break-even for 7 days | Pause ad, test new hook or product set |
| ROAS | Below target after 50+ purchases | Cut budget, review listing price and images |
| Outbound CTR | Under ~1% | Change creative |
| Frequency (retargeting) | Above 4 in 7 days | Refresh creative or widen window |
| Ad set at ~50 purchases/week | Reached | Move to ROAS goal or cost cap |
