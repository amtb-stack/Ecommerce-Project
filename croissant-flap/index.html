            How to Play

            Fly the croissant through the gaps. Each gap is 1 point. Don't hit the bread or the counter!

            On a computer

            • Space, ↑, W or click to flap
            • P or Esc to pause
            • M to mute

            On a phone or tablet

            • Tap anywhere to flap
            • Use the buttons at the top to pause or mute
