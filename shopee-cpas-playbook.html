Shopee CPAS Playbook for Ovens and Deep Fryers

Campaign, ad set and ad setup for commercial kitchen equipment, plus what the creative should look like. Written for Shopee Malaysia with the ringgit amounts below as examples. Confirm formats and attribution settings with your Shopee CPAS contact, since Meta and Shopee change them often.

Step 0

Before you create anything

With CPAS, Shopee holds the catalog and the checkout. You buy the Meta ad, and the sale happens on Shopee. That changes what you control: the ad creative is mostly built from your Shopee product images, titles and prices.

  • Shopee sideSeller account enrolled in the Shopee CPAS programme, with your account manager or the CPAS onboarding form.
  • Meta sideBusiness Manager, Facebook Page, an ad account, and partner access shared with Shopee's Business Manager.
  • CatalogYour products synced into the shared catalog. Group them into three product sets: Ovens, Deep Fryers, Bundles.
  • TrackingPurchases are measured by Shopee's data, not your own pixel. Ask how the order and GMV reports reach you.

Ad

Ad 1 · Catalog carouselDynamic cards for each product in the set. Your workhorse.

Retargeting adsSame three formats. Headline changes to "Still looking?" and the frame shows price drops.

Oven and fryer orders are high value, so Shopee will record fewer purchases than for small items. If your total budget is under about RM100 a day, start with two ad sets in C1 (Ovens and Fryers, bundles inside both) and split out the third later.

Step 3

What the creative should look like

In CPAS the catalog builds most of the ad, so the quality of your Shopee listing is your creative. Fix the listing first, then add the frame and a video.

Main product image

  • 1080 × 1080, square
  • Clean light background, no text or watermark
  • Machine fills about 80% of the frame
  • Show the front with door or baskets visible

Extra images in the listing

  • In use: fried chicken in the basket, trays of bread
  • Size reference with a person or a standard tray
  • Capacity and power spec image (litres, kW, voltage)

Product title and price

  • Put the key words first: "Commercial Deep Fryer 2×6L Electric"
  • Only about 35 characters show on a card
  • Keep sale price and original price correct, mismatches get ads rejected

Frame and badges

  • One accent colour, matching your brand
  • Price badge, discount %, "Free delivery" strip
  • Keep it to three elements, so the product stays the hero

Video for the collection cover

  • 9:16 or 4:5, 10 to 15 seconds
  • First 2 seconds: golden food coming out of the machine
  • Then the machine, then one claim (capacity, speed or oil saving)
  • Burn in captions, many people watch muted

Primary text

  • One line on the pain point: running cost, speed, consistency
  • One line on proof: warranty, rating, units sold
  • Call to action: Shop Now

Visual mockups

Mockups with example products and prices. Replace "Your Brand" and the numbers with your own listing.

Do

  • Show food in at least one image per product
  • Keep capacity and power visible, buyers compare specs
  • Refresh frames and video every 2 to 3 weeks
  • Name the buyer in the primary text: café, bakery, hawker stall

Don't

  • Crowd the product with text, badges or logos
  • Use blurry supplier photos or stock images of other brands
  • Promise results you cannot show, such as "save 50% oil"
  • Leave sale badges up after the promotion ends
Step 4

Launch and review

Days 1 to 7Launch C1 with all ad sets and three ads each. Change nothing.
Week 2Turn off the weakest ad in each ad set by cost per purchase. Add a new hook.
Week 3Launch C2 once Shopee shows enough viewers and cart adders to retarget.
12.12 and year-endSwitch on C3 with sale frames. Raise budget 3x for the 3 to 5 days around the event.
