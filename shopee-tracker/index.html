    Backup

    Tickets are stored on this device only. Your phone and your computer keep separate boards. Clearing browser data wipes them.

    Load a file from your other device

    Merge keeps every ticket from both devices. If the same ticket changed in two places, the most recent edit wins and both sets of history are kept.

    Keeping two devices in step

    Save the .json into Google Drive or Dropbox from the device you just used, then load it on the other one before you start working there. Do it when you switch devices, not continuously — and never work on both at once with an old file in between.

    Alerts that reach a locked phone

    This app can only pop up while it's open. To be interrupted when it's closed, the reminder has to live in your calendar or your clock app — those are the only things allowed to wake a locked phone.

    1. Daily check-in reminders — set this up once

    Three repeating events at your check-in times, each with an alarm. Import once and it reminds you every day, forever, whether or not this app is open.

    2. Deadlines for tickets open right now

    One calendar alarm per open ticket, 30 minutes before its deadline and again when it hits. This is a snapshot — closed tickets leave the old event behind, so use it for the ones you really can't miss, not every day.

    How to get the file into your calendar

    Most reliable: download on your computer, go to calendar.google.com → gear icon → Settings → Import & export → Import. It syncs to your phone by itself within a minute.

    iPhone: tapping the downloaded file opens it straight in the Calendar app — tap Add All.
    Android: the Google Calendar app can't import files directly. Email the file to yourself and open it on the phone, or use the computer route above.

    Or just use your clock app

    Three repeating alarms labelled "Check board" costs nothing, takes two minutes, and is the single most reliable reminder on any phone. Do this even if you also import the calendar file.
