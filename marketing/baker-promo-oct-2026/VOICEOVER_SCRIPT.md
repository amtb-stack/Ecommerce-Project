# The Baker Bundle Promo: voiceover script (40 s, cinematic trailer read)

**Style:** epic movie-trailer narrator. Deep, slow, steady, full of awe, never salesy. Let the silences do the work.
**Length:** about 50 words in 40 s, with long pauses (…) between lines. Don't rush the numbers.
**Delivery:** voice only (`out/voiceover_40s_en.wav` / `.mp3`, exactly 40.0 s). The guide voice is pitched down, warmed and put in a hall reverb. Regenerate with `python3 voiceover.py <piper-voice.onnx> out/voiceover_40s_en.wav`.

## Plain read (EN)

> In every kitchen…
> there comes a moment… when one is simply not enough.
> A one-hundred-litre oven.
> Two hundred and fifty degrees… of pure heat.
> And beside it… fifteen hundred watts… of power.
> Together… they are unstoppable.
> Buy both. And get so much more.
> Welcome… to The Baker Bundle.
> Only on Shopee.

## Notes for a voice artist

* Aim for a low register with a chest voice, and treat each line as its own moment.
* Rise slightly on "In every kitchen…", then drop on "not enough."
* Hold "Welcome…" for a full beat before "to The Baker Bundle."
* If you add a price later, replace line 8 with: **"Both… for just RM ___."** (BM: **"Kedua-duanya… hanya RM ___."**)
